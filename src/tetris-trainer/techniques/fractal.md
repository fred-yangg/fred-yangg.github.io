# Fractal

Status: draft, not reviewed.
Sources: [four.lol](https://four.lol/methods/fractal), [Hard Drop wiki](https://harddrop.com/wiki/Fractal).

## What it is

Two TSD slots stacked so the first TSD opens the second: TSD → TSD. Also
called "double dagger" (Japanese community) or Spider. It can be stacked
higher, but three or more is rare.

```
Shape                 First TSD             Second TSD
###.......            ###.......            ..........
##...#####            ##TTT#####            ###.......
###.######            ###T######            ##TTT#####
##...#####            ##...#####            ###T######
###.######            ###.######
```

- **Fractal 1:** the two slots line up evenly, one above the other.
- **Fractal 2:** the shape is uneven. The upper overhang has to be on the
  correct side or it blocks the lower slot.
- **With a gap:** there can be rows between the two slots.

## When to use it

- You don't expect a T soon. A Fractal stores two TSDs, so the stack keeps its
  shape until the Ts arrive.
- There's already a TSD slot. Building on top of it is the most common and
  easiest way to make a Fractal.
- Compared with a DT Cannon, a Fractal is usually better when there's an extra
  row underneath.

When not to use it:

- It needs two Ts, and it's hard to build without using Ts in the stack. Both
  sources warn that a T drought makes it risky.
- The common mistake is putting the upper overhang on the wrong side.
- Moving the overhang turns it into an Imperial Cross, a different TSD → TSD.

## Starting points

1. **On top of an existing TSD.** Place the upper slot's pieces above a TSD
   slot that's already there. Repeating L, S, J, Z is the usual pattern, and is
   the basis of LST stacking.
2. **STMB Cave over an incomplete TSD (Fractal Cave).** Common after the TST in
   Triple Double openers.
3. **LT stacking.** Repeat L on one side and use the T for the TSD. The cleanup
   after both TSDs is tricky, so it's rare.
4. **J/L prop.** Needs more planning of the pieces, similar to building a DT
   Cannon.

```
On top of a TSD (S and Z build the upper slot)
#.........
##........
##..S#####
#...SS####
##.ZZS####
#...ZZ####
##.#######
##.#######
```

## Pieces

- Two Ts, one for each TSD.
- The upper slot's overhang and fill. L, S, J and Z are the usual ones (LST
  stacking), and other pieces can stand in.

## What it leaves

Four rows cleared. What's left depends on how the upper slot was built. LT
stacking leaves an L block on its column, which can be useful later.

## How the trainer should test it

- **Start:** a stack with one TSD slot (or an STMB-cave-ready shape) and room
  to build a second slot above it. The preview has a T coming, then another
  one a bag later.
- **Goal:** TSD → TSD, where the second slot is the one the first TSD opens,
  finishing clean.
- **Wrong-call positions:** overhang on the wrong side (the second TSD gets
  blocked), Imperial Cross being the better shape, or no second T in reach.
- **Built so far:** two stacked slots with the top two or three pieces taken
  back, and both Ts close together in the preview. Wrong calls come up when
  another pair of TSDs finishes cleaner. Not yet: STMB caves, the second T a
  bag later, and the overhang-on-the-wrong-side trap.
